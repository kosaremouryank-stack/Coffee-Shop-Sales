# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee shop transaction data using **Microsoft Excel** to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, store-wise revenue, product demand, and time-based sales trends. The workbook contains cleaned transaction-level data, pivot summaries, and a dashboard-ready structure for business analysis.

## Dataset Description

The dataset contains **149,116 transactions** from coffee shop stores across different locations. It includes transaction details such as date, time, store location, product category, product type, quantity sold, unit price, and total bill amount.

### Key Columns

- `transaction_id` — Unique transaction identifier
- `transaction_date` — Date of transaction
- `transaction_time` — Time of transaction
- `store_location` — Store branch location
- `product_category` — Product category such as Coffee, Tea, Bakery, etc.
- `product_type` — Type of product sold
- `product_detail` — Specific product name
- `transaction_qty` — Quantity purchased
- `unit_price` — Price per unit
- `Total_bill` — Total sales amount
- `Month Name` — Month of transaction
- `Day Name` — Day of transaction
- `Hour` — Hour of transaction

## Key Metrics

- **Total Transactions:** 149,116
- **Total Revenue:** ₹698,496
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 214,470 units
- **Top Store by Revenue:** Hell's Kitchen
- **Top Product Category:** Coffee
- **Highest Revenue Month:** June
- **Most Active Day:** Friday
- **Peak Sales Hour:** 10 AM

## Business Insights

- Coffee generated the highest revenue, followed by Tea and Bakery products.
- Hell's Kitchen was the best-performing store by revenue.
- June recorded the highest monthly sales, showing strong growth compared to previous months.
- Friday had the highest number of transactions, making it the busiest day of the week.
- Morning hours, especially 8 AM to 10 AM, showed strong customer demand.
- Premium coffee products such as Ethiopia, Sustainably Grown Organic, and Jamaican Coffee River contributed significantly to revenue.

## Analysis Performed

- Cleaned and structured transaction data for analysis.
- Created calculated fields such as total bill, month, day, hour, and day of week.
- Built pivot tables to summarize sales by month, day, hour, store, and product category.
- Compared store-wise performance to identify top-performing branches.
- Analyzed product categories to understand revenue contribution.
- Identified peak sales hours and high-demand time periods.
